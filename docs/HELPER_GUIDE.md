# Helper guide

Guided self-help works better than self-help alone. A **helper** is a trained non-specialist (e.g. community health worker, peer supporter, psychology graduate) who gives each participant one short call or message exchange per week. The helper does **not** deliver therapy — they encourage, troubleshoot, and keep people safe.

Set `helper.enabled: true` and `helper.contactDetails` in `assets/js/config.js` so participants see how to reach you.

## Role

**Do:** encourage, praise effort, help solve practical barriers to doing the sessions and activities, explain content if unclear, check safety every week, refer on when needed.
**Don't:** give advice on personal decisions, diagnose, counsel in depth, share your own problems, promise confidentiality without limits.

## Basic skills

- Warm, unhurried tone; use their name.
- Open questions: "What was that like?" "What got in the way?"
- Reflect back: "It sounds like the market trip felt like too much."
- Praise effort, not just results: "You tried even though you were tired — that matters."
- Keep to time (15–20 minutes).

## Call structure (every week)

1. **Greeting & check-in** (2 min) — "How has your week been?"
2. **Safety** (1–2 min) — always ask: "Some people feeling low have thoughts that life isn't worth living, or of ending their life. Have you had thoughts like that this week?" If yes → follow `SAFETY_PROTOCOL.md` before anything else.
3. **Session progress** (5 min) — Did they complete this week's session? What did they find useful? Anything confusing?
4. **Practice review** (5 min) — Which planned activities happened? How did they feel afterwards? For missed ones: what got in the way; how could it be made smaller?
5. **Next week** (2 min) — Confirm the next session; one specific plan; time of next call.
6. **Close** — Encouragement; remind them of urgent help.

## Session-specific prompts

| Week | Focus on |
|---|---|
| After Session 0 | Did they manage to start? Anything worrying about the programme? Remind: one session a week. |
| After Session 1 | Have they planned activities small enough? Reinforce "action first, motivation follows." |
| After Session 2 | Breathing practised daily? Practise together for one minute on the call if helpful. Task breakdown. |
| After Session 3 | Ask for one harsh thought and help them find a kinder one. Watch for strong hopelessness (safety). |
| After Session 4 | Did they make contact? If it went badly, normalise and plan a gentler step. |
| After Session 5 | Celebrate. Review staying-well plan; check it includes a professional contact. Close support. |

## When someone is struggling to engage

- Missed sessions: no blame — "Life gets in the way. What would make it easier this week?" Offer to go through part of it on the call.
- No change after 2–3 weeks, or getting worse: discuss with your supervisor; consider referral.
- Can't read well: use the paper workbook with them, or read the story aloud on the call.

## Supervision

Helpers need weekly group or individual supervision from a mental-health professional, a clear referral pathway, and a written record (date, safety answer, progress, actions) for every contact. Keep records securely.
